Details behind the numbers

Ceilings: labor is 20% higher than matching wall work. Ceiling rows and a complete walls-and-ceiling total are included above. The earlier wall-only rows still exclude the ceiling.

Finishing includes seams, inside corners, screw heads, coating and sanding ready for primer.

Room calculations: no deductions for doors or windows. Sheets are estimated by wall area divided by 32 and rounded up; actual layout can require more. Existing seam totals assume horizontal 4 × 8 sheets and include inside corners.

Paint estimate: two coats at 350 sq. ft. per gallon per coat, rounded up to whole gallons. Primer material is not included.

$150 service minimum: shared across work on the visit. The finishing proposal covers its required repeat coats and visits; no new minimum is added per coat in this chart.

Repair difference: a $450 full-sheet patch includes work around existing surfaces. Hanging and finishing one new sheet within a larger job works out to $139. Multiple repairs together still need a combined quote; no discount schedule has been approved.

Profit: these are customer charges. Your labor hours, materials, supplies and travel costs must be subtracted to determine profit.
