Reviewed installation scopes

Interior slab door installation includes fitting, hinge mortising, lockset preparation and strike-plate preparation in a usable existing frame. Do not add a separate mortising service to the slab-door installation. New frames and opening alterations are separate.

Attic access ladder replacement includes removal of the old ladder and installation in the existing opening. New attic access ladder installation includes cutting and framing a new opening in a suitable drywall ceiling. Ladder and materials are separate; structural alterations beyond the standard opening need an in-person estimate.

Kitchen sink hookup includes the drain connection using existing compatible plumbing. Faucet installation is a separate service. Sink removal and haul-off includes drain disconnection.

The tile backsplash starting price covers a basic 15-square-foot installation on a ready wall, including grouting. Tile and supplies, old backsplash removal, wall repairs, larger areas and complicated patterns are separate. All countertop installation and removal require an in-person estimate.

Garage door insulation starting labor prices cover a standard kit on a single-car or double-car door as selected. The kit and materials are separate.

Ceiling fan installation using existing connections covers a standard fan or light replacement with suitable existing support. Support upgrades and ceiling repairs cost extra and are confirmed before work. New wiring is priced separately under the new-wiring service.

Under-cabinet lighting using existing connections accounts for replacing existing lights. New wiring and switch work assumes a straightforward accessible installation. Difficult access, a new circuit and wall repairs require separate pricing. Heated towel rack prices distinguish plug-in, existing hardwired and new-wiring installations; unit and materials are separate.

Electric wall heater replacement requires a compatible existing opening and wiring. Standard infrared heater mounting uses existing compatible power. New wiring, new recessed openings and associated repairs need an in-person estimate.

Door removal and haul-off

Same-visit door removal rates: one door $150; two doors $100 each; three doors $85 each; four or more $75 each. The cart combines door quantities across rooms. Window removal and haul-off is $250 per window. Appliance removal stays $150 per appliance. A stacked washer/dryer contains two appliances, so removal and haul-off is $300 for the set. Cabinet removal keeps its separate quantity schedule. The $150 minimum trip charge is shared across work on the visit.

Labor and materials

Labor prices exclude materials and fixtures unless specifically included. Toilet tank repair parts are not included.

Minimum trip charge

A $150 minimum applies to the service visit, shared across work performed during that visit.

Bathroom exhaust fans

The starting price for installation with new duct covers ductwork routed through the attic. Venting through an exterior wall costs extra. Exhaust must terminate outdoors; it is not discharged into the attic.

Speaker installation

Standard speaker installation uses existing compatible wiring. Wall-mounted installation includes securing the bracket to suitable wall support, attaching the speaker, connecting the existing wire and testing operation. Built-in installation includes fitting the speaker opening, connecting existing wiring, securing the speaker and testing. Speakers, brackets and materials are separate. New wiring, full speaker systems, structural changes and repairs require an in-person estimate. A $150 minimum trip charge applies across work on the visit, not separately to each speaker.

Smart device installation

Standard smart light switch, thermostat and doorbell installation uses compatible existing connections and includes basic app or hub pairing and testing of the installed device. Device purchase is separate. Basic setup is included in the installation price and is not charged again as a separate setup service. New wiring, connection changes, network troubleshooting, whole-home lighting systems, multiple hubs and advanced routines require a separate in-person estimate.

Ceiling labor

Ceiling drywall installation, finishing, priming and painting labor is 20% higher than the corresponding wall labor. The same package discounts apply before the ceiling premium. Materials are priced separately. Drywall patch prices include finishing; priming and painting are separate.

Cabinet removal

Removal and haul-off: 1–2 cabinets $75 each; 3–5 $65 each; 6–9 $55 each; 10 or more $50 each. The $150 minimum trip charge applies. Countertops and plumbing disconnections are separate.
