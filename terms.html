Terms, Conditions & Policies

Labor and materials

Labor prices exclude materials and fixtures unless specifically included. Toilet tank repair parts are not included.

Minimum service charge

A $150 minimum applies to the service visit, shared across work performed during that visit.

Bathroom exhaust fans

The starting price for installation with new duct covers ductwork routed through the attic. Venting through an exterior wall costs extra. Exhaust must terminate outdoors; it is not discharged into the attic.

Speaker installation

Standard speaker installation uses existing compatible wiring. Wall-mounted installation includes securing the bracket to suitable wall support, attaching the speaker, connecting the existing wire and testing operation. Built-in installation includes fitting the speaker opening, connecting existing wiring, securing the speaker and testing. Speakers, brackets and materials are separate. New wiring, full speaker systems, structural changes and repairs require an in-person estimate. A $150 minimum trip charge applies across work on the visit, not separately to each speaker.

Smart device installation

Standard smart light switch, thermostat and doorbell installation uses compatible existing connections and includes basic app or hub pairing and testing of the installed device. Device purchase is separate. Basic setup is included in the installation price and is not charged again as a separate setup service. New wiring, connection changes, network troubleshooting, whole-home lighting systems, multiple hubs and advanced routines require a separate in-person estimate.

Ceiling labor

Ceiling drywall installation, finishing, priming and painting labor is 20% higher than the corresponding wall labor. The same package discounts apply before the ceiling premium. Materials are priced separately. Drywall patch prices include finishing; priming and painting are separate.

Cabinet removal

Removal and haul-off: 1–2 cabinets $75 each; 3–5 $65 each; 6–9 $55 each; 10 or more $50 each. The $150 visit minimum applies. Countertops and plumbing disconnections are separate.
