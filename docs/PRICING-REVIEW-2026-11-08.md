## Calculation and safety

Owner labor is $75/hour or approved fixed task amounts. Owner material references already include the 35% markup. This draft adds a **proposed 10% planning/fees allowance**, deducts **7% from the entire package including fees**, then rounds UP to the next $5. Taxes, shipping and unexpected repairs are not included. The owner must approve all scopes and the fee allowance.
