## Calculation rule (owner review)

- Labor $75/hr unless an approved fixed task price is more appropriate; $150 minimum visit.
- Material references already include the target **35% markup**. Never add that markup twice. Tools are not charged as consumables automatically.
- Default package calculation for this review: (labor + materials + supplies + 10% *planning fee*) less **7% discount on the entire sum**, rounded **up** to the next $5. The 10% planning fee is inherited as a *proposed* allowance and is not an owner-approved standalone charge.
- Tax/shipping, hidden repairs, structural modifications and licensed trades outside the defined scope are **not** included in these draft totals.
- Deposits are planning splits only: materials and supplies upfront, remaining labor and fees paid later subject to signed agreement. Never charge a draft price.
- The *starting price* assumes the specific minimum dimensions and scope in each scenario. A larger customer room is not automatically sold at the same price.

## No safe public starting price yet (review checklist)

These project categories need a product selection, a minimum scope, or safety/structural site assessment. Price a smaller defined tier if appropriate; do not advertise a zero or speculative figure.

- **backyard-retreat:** Pergola/gazebo footing size, kit product and weather-rated assembly not selected.
- **bathrooms:** Existing legacy Basic/Standard/Deluxe site prices need component audit; missing priced fixtures, sizes, removal terms and painting component breakdown. Do not overwrite approved numbers until reconciled.
- **bus-stop:** Shelter footing, local accessibility/siting code and structural plans missing.
- **car-wash:** Drainage/water handling, containment and wash equipment are site-specific.
- **concrete:** Outdoor Projects acts as category; price individual safe scopes only.
- **custom:** Open-ended specialty work cannot have meaningful starting package total.
- **decks-railings:** Structural design, load ratings, fasteners and full deck assembly labor require site-specific estimate.
- **entry-mudroom:** Bench, door hardware/threshold and mounting details vary; source exact bench and compatible hardware before advertising complete package.
- **exterior-improvements:** Exterior paint product, prep, height/access and waterproofing specifications incomplete.
- **fitness-wellness:** Gym floor product, mirror anchoring, substrate/weight-bearing review and ventilation equipment remain unspecified.
- **games-hobbies:** Customer-specific worktable/game table and specialty equipment costs unresolved.
- **garden-greenhouse:** Kit size, foundation, treated lumber, soil and required anchoring vary.
- **kitchen-pantry:** Existing legacy Basic/Standard/Deluxe site prices need reconciliation; cabinets/counters selection, sink, faucet, backsplash, removal and tax split are not fully costed.
- **outdoor-kitchen:** Outdoor cabinet, countertop, grill and plumbing/electrical products not specified.
- **outdoor-sound:** Equipment is customer-specific, weather-rated and often electrical; choose exact parts before pricing.
- **patios-concrete:** New slab requires concrete yardage, access, base, forms, reinforcement and site prep; existing paint use depends on exterior coating specs.
- **pet-room:** Washable coating, kennel materials and pet door/weather seals not yet specified.
- **playground:** Equipment kit manufacturer/anchoring and safety surfacing need verified specification.
- **pools:** Pool equipment and safety requirements require pool contractor coordination; no equipment quote.
- **porches-entryways:** Stairs/rails/door condition may be structural/weatherproof; site assessment.
- **room-additions:** Permits, engineering, foundation, framing, electrical/HVAC, trades and structural assessment mandatory.
- **snack-beverage:** Countertop surface, cabinets and lighting model unselected; water/plumbing changes require quote.
- **stairs:** Handrail and tread safety-critical condition; lumber, railing and stair components not priced as installed assembly.
- **themed-rooms:** Category landing page; apply prices to individual selected themed projects.
- **vault-safe-room:** Structural/security design, safe anchoring, doors and ventilation require on-site assessment.
- **wallpaper:** Wallpaper double-roll material, repeat/waste and paste type not selected; labor alone is $75/double roll standard, $90 specialty.
